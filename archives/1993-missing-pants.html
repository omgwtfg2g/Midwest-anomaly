MAN SAYS ALIENS RETURNED HIS LAWN MOWER, KEPT HIS PANTS

Alleged abductee demands explanation after visitors drop off equipment, issue receipt and depart with his trousers.
BY THE MIDWEST ANOMALY · OCTOBER 24, 1993
Artist's reconstruction of the alleged craft. The witness declined to pose for a photograph below the waist.

LOWELL, MICH. — A Kent County resident who says he was taken aboard a flying saucer Thursday night is demanding the return of his trousers, which he believes were retained by extraterrestrial visitors as part of a poorly explained exchange program.

The 41-year-old man says a brilliant light appeared above his garage shortly after 11 p.m. He remembers being lifted into the air alongside his riding lawn mower, a garden hose and one frightened raccoon.

“I thought they wanted the mower,” he said. “I told them the transmission slips. They didn't seem concerned. Then they started looking at me.”

According to the account, three small gray beings examined the mower for nearly an hour before conducting what the man called a “deeply personal inventory” of his clothing.

“I got the mower back. I got the hose back. Even the raccoon came home. Where are my pants?”

He said the visitors returned him to his driveway at 3:17 a.m. wearing a hospital gown made of material that crackled when he moved. The mower was neatly parked beside him, its gas tank filled and blades sharpened.

His trousers were nowhere to be found.

“They handed me a receipt,” he said. “It's got six symbols on it and what looks like a smiley face. I don't know who I'm supposed to call.”

The man's wife said she initially doubted the account but became concerned when he refused to sit near the living room window. “He keeps saying they're coming back to finish the paperwork,” she said.

A neighbor described seeing an unusual light, although he conceded it might have been the headlights of a passing combine.

The resident says he has no intention of seeking a second encounter. “If they want a follow-up appointment, they can send a letter,” he said. “And they'd better include my pants.”

Asked whether the aliens had at least repaired the mower's slipping transmission, he shook his head. “No. That's the part that really bothers me.”
