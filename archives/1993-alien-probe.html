LOCAL MAN CLAIMS ALIENS PROBED WRONG END, DEMANDS FORMAL APOLOGY

Resident says late-night visitors offered no identification, no paperwork and absolutely no bedside manner.
BY THE MIDWEST ANOMALY · OCTOBER 23, 1993
Illustration depicting the reported visitors. No photographs of the encounter were produced.

GRAND RAPIDS, MICH. — A local man has filed what he describes as an official complaint against unidentified extraterrestrials after an alleged late-night encounter left him confused, embarrassed and deeply dissatisfied with the quality of care.

According to the man's account, three small gray visitors entered his bedroom shortly after 2 a.m., communicated entirely through blinking, and informed him that he had been selected for “an important scientific procedure.”

“I said I had to work at seven,” the man recalled. “They said the appointment had already been scheduled. I asked by who. They just blinked faster.”

The alleged examination reportedly took place aboard a circular craft hovering over a nearby cornfield. The man described a spotless room, a bright overhead light and a clipboard that appeared to be made of liquid metal.

“I've had better service at the Secretary of State.”

The resident claims the visitors used an unfamiliar instrument during the examination, then refused to explain what it measured. When he requested a copy of his medical records, one alien allegedly handed him a small glowing cube that played a recording of a cow mooing.

“I don't even know if my insurance covers this,” he said. “They never asked for my card.”

Neighbors reported no unusual activity, although one resident recalled hearing a lawn mower start at approximately 3:15 a.m. despite the absence of anyone mowing.

The man has demanded a written apology, reimbursement for a missing pair of slippers, and confirmation that his name has been removed from what he believes is an extraterrestrial follow-up mailing list.

His final concern remains unanswered: “They told me to come back in six months. Come back where?”
